Onboarding Dashboard
If new customer opens their Orders Hub dashboard.

Instead of seeing an empty dashboard, they see:

Welcome to Orders Hub! [Welcome tutorial]

Your store setup progress:
- [x] Create your business account.
- [ ] Complete your first business profile. [button to open `Create a business` modal] [create business tutorial]
- [ ] Add your first product. [button to open `New product` modal] [create product tutorial]
- [ ] Share your store/product link. [button to open `New product` modal]
- [ ] Receive your first order.[accept order tutorial]
Progress: 1/5 completed.

The dashboard should guide the customer toward the next meaningful action.

This is much better than showing dozens of features without explaining what to do first.
